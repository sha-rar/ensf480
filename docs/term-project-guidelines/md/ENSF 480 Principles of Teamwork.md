Software is a Teamwork Product

ò Software development relies on management or dealing with 4 Ps:
ò People: customers, developers, users, mangers, etc. In general, called

stakeholder

ò Process: phases of activities to convert an idea to a product
ò Project: A planed undertaking that must be delivered at a deadline to achieve a

goal

ò Product: An article or substance that is manufactured, build, or refined,

normally for sale.

What is Software Team

ò Development teams work together to build a software product.
Key to the success of the team is to be self-organized and cross-
functional.

ò Team members should make a collaborative effort when comes

to communication and conducting shared responsibilities.

ò Every  person  on  the  team  should  play  a  part  and  be  held

accountable in making the production happen.

Team Synergy

ò Successful start up techs look for a self-managed technical team who are heartedly

dedicated to their work.

ò Analytical skill: System thinking
ò Technical skill

Team/Group Constitution

ò The constitution deals with:

ò the rights, powers and obligations of all people involved in the group.
ò Sets out clear guidelines as to what conduct and behavior is acceptable

and what is unacceptable.

ò The constitution cannot extend the legal capacity, rights, powers

and privileges that the group would otherwise have.

Difficulties When Working as a Team

ò The biggest problem hindering group progress is
misunderstanding and miscommunication. E.g.:

ò What are the goals and objectives?
ò What is required?
ò Who is responsible?
ò What do we do in given situations?
ò When should I do act?

ò Another problem is conflicting expectations.

ò What do we expect to accomplish as the project proceeds?

ò  Some membersÆ understanding of the project might not be in harmony with

others.

Group Constitution Contents
ò Establish common goals and standards for the project team.

ò A brief statement of the goals of the group.

ò What do we wish to accomplish? Example (As a university student): To work industrious and diligent to accomplish

the requirements of the course

ò Similar to ôStatement of Purpose,ö but for group not project.

ò What is the format of documents? Normally companies and enterprises try to use a uniform format for technical

documents (e.g., Text font size should be at least 12 points, All reports must have a cover page)

ò What tools will be used?

ò Example: StarUML, Visio, etc.

ò Documents, assignments, working disks:

ò Where will they be stored ?
ò Who will have access?
ò Back up procedures?

ò How do we contact each other?

ò phone numbers, email addresses, etc.

ò Discussions with instructor should always be done as a group.
